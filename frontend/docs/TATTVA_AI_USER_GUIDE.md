## 1. Quick Start

### Navigating the Platform
- **AI Chat (`/chat`):** Real-time conversational interface with multi-modal understanding, file attachments, and on-demand Read Aloud.
- **Evaluation Lab (`/evaluation`):** Empirical MMMLU Hindi benchmarking across 57 academic subjects.
- **Beta Portal (`/beta`):** Tester activation and structured bug/feedback intake.
- **Settings (`/settings`):** Manage notification alerts, theme appearance, and encrypted BYOK API keys.

---

## 2. Using Core Features

### Multi-Modal AI Chat
- **Text & Code:** Ask programming questions in Python, TypeScript, C++, Rust, and SQL.
- **Multilingual Queries:** Converse naturally in English, Hindi, and Telugu.
- **File Uploads:** Click the `+` button to attach documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT) up to 25MB for immediate analysis.
- **Presentation Synthesis:** Type `"Create a 5-slide presentation on renewable solar energy in India"` to download a native `.pptx` presentation file.

### Document Intelligence & RAG
- Upload documents to your personal workspace.
- TATTVA AI compresses relevant context by 73%, delivering fast answers with exact citations.

### Privacy & Account Controls
- Your private conversations are **never** used to train AI models.
- You can export your data or execute a complete GDPR Right-To-Be-Forgotten purge at any time in `/settings`.
